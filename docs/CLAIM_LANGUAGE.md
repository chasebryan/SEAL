# Claim Language

Acceptable v0 language:

- SEAL-Core v0 is a terminal-native normative authority core.
- The trusted authority model is written in F*/Low*.
- The v0 F* authority gate verifies when `make verify` succeeds.
- The seL4 mapping is conceptual only in v0.

Prohibited v0 language must only appear as an explicit denial or non-claim:

- not production ready
- not externally certified
- not government approved
- not seL4 equivalent
- not formally verified as a whole system

Required non-claim language:

- not production authorized
- not externally certified
- not government approved
- not seL4-equivalent
- not formally verified as a whole system
